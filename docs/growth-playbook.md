# Growth playbook: a sample week

A one-week plan for a small to mid-size account (roughly 200–5,000 followers)
that wants real reach, not vanity numbers. Repeat it weekly and adjust with
the metrics in section 4.

## 0. Before week 1: account foundations (about 60 minutes, once)

| Item | Checklist |
|---|---|
| Avatar | Clear face or logo, high contrast, readable at small size |
| Display name | Name + short descriptor if useful ("Lan · Pricing for SaaS") |
| Bio | Who you help, with what, one proof point. No hashtag lists |
| Link | One CTA only: newsletter, product, or booking page |
| Header | Repeats the bio promise visually |
| Pinned post | Best thread, or "who I am and why follow" post |
| Apps | Settings > Security and account access > Apps and sessions: revoke tools you don't use (especially auto-follow / auto-DM tools) |

## 1. Weekly rhythm

| Day | Original posts | Thread | Replies to larger accounts | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 1 (opinion + reason) | – | 5 | Plan the week's 3 themes |
| Tue | 1 (how-to, numbered) | 1 (framework or case study) | 5 | Reply to every genuine comment within 1 hour |
| Wed | 1 (data or result) | – | 5 | 1 quote post that adds your take |
| Thu | 1 (short video < 60 s, captions) | – | 5 | – |
| Fri | 1 (lesson learned / mistake) | – | 5 | Engage with 10 peer accounts' posts meaningfully |
| Sat | 1 (light / behind the scenes) | – | optional | – |
| Sun | – | – | – | Weekly review (section 4), refresh pinned post if a better post appeared |

Totals: 6 originals, 1 thread, ~25 replies, 1 quote post, 1 video.

### Replies that grow an account

- Pick 10–15 larger accounts (5–50× your size) in your niche; turn on notifications for a few.
- Reply within the first 30–60 minutes, when replies are seen the most.
- Each reply adds one of: a data point, a concrete example, a respectful counterpoint, a short how-to.
- Never: "Great post!", self-promotion links, the same reply pasted under several posts (that last one is a spam signal).

## 2. Content formula

```
Hook:   one line that makes "show more" worth it (number, contrast, promise)
Proof:  data, your result, a source, an example
Action: what the reader should do or think next
Link:   last, or in the first reply
```

Example:

> I cut our SaaS churn from 7% to 4% in 90 days without discounts.
>
> 1. Called every user who cancelled in month 1 (31 calls).
> 2. 18 of them never finished setup, so we added a 5-minute onboarding call.
> 3. Monthly churn: 7.1% → 4.2%.
>
> If you sell software: check how many churned users finished setup.
> Details in the reply.

## 3. Fake traffic: do not do

| Tactic | Why not |
|---|---|
| Buying followers / likes / views | Against platform-manipulation rules; dead audience lowers every conversion rate |
| Follow/unfollow churn, F4F, follow trains | Spam signal; triggers action limits |
| Engagement pods | Coordinated inauthentic behaviour; skews your data |
| Reply/DM bots, auto-like tools | Automation rules; common cause of locks and captchas |
| Hashtag stuffing, trend hijacking | Looks like spam; attracts irrelevant viewers |
| "Follow + RT + tag 3" giveaways | Followers who leave the day after |
| Several of your own accounts boosting each other | Platform manipulation |

## 4. Weekly review (Sunday, 20 minutes)

Write the numbers into a sheet every week:

Profile visits and link clicks come from account analytics, which X currently
limits to Premium subscribers. Without Premium, track per-post views (shown
under each post), follower change and replies, and skip the rows you cannot fill.

| Metric | Formula | Rough benchmark* | If low, try |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impression → profile visit | profile visits / impressions | 0.5–2% | Stronger hooks; posts that show expertise, not just opinions |
| Profile visit → follow | new follows / profile visits | 5–15% | Fix bio and pinned post |
| Follow → engagement within 7 days | new followers who liked/replied within 7 days / new followers | 10%+ | Ask a specific question in the next 2 posts |
| Link click rate | link clicks / impressions on link posts | 0.5–1.5% | Put the link in the reply; state the benefit of clicking |

\*Benchmarks are rough starting points from small and mid-size accounts. Your
own week-over-week trend is the better guide.

Rules for reading the numbers:

- Judge after 7 days, not per post or per day.
- Compare with the same number of posts. Fewer posts = fewer impressions.
- A sudden drop to near zero **while posting normally** is when to run the
  limit check (`docs/rate-limit.md`).

## 5. Three things this week (default when there is no data)

1. Fix the foundations in section 0 and pin your best post.
2. Publish 6 originals + 1 thread using the formula in section 2.
3. Leave 5 high-quality replies per weekday on larger accounts, within 30–60 minutes of their posts.

Expectation: profile visits per 1,000 impressions and follows per 100 profile
visits should rise over 2–3 weeks. If they don't, change the hook style first,
then the topics.
